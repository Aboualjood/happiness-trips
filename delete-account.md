If you wish to delete your Happiness Trips account and all associated personal data, you can submit a deletion request at any time.

How to request account deletion: Send an email to happinesstrips30@gmail.com from the email address associated with your Happiness Trips account.

Use the subject line: "Account Deletion Request - Happiness Trips".

In the body of the email, simply state that you want your account and data deleted.

What data will be deleted: Once we receive and verify your request, we will permanently delete the following data within 14 days:

Your account profile (Name and Email address).

Your app activity (Saved properties and favorites).

Data retention: Standard server crash logs or anonymous performance analytics may be retained temporarily (up to 90 days) for security and debugging purposes, but this data will no longer be linked to your personal identity.
